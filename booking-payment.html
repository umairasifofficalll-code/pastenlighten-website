Our prices

Every tour on the website shows the price for a solo traveller, per person in US dollars, from US$40 (Lahore evening tours) to US$4,290 (16-day Grand Northern Tour). Travelling with 2 or more people? The price per person is lower, and your quote shows the exact group price. Prices include an English-speaking guide and all entry tickets at the foreigner rate. See all tours and prices.
