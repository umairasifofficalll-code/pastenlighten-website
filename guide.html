Meet your guide

About Bilawal & Past Enlighten

A Lahore-born guide sharing the Walled City he grew up in, and the valleys of the north, with travellers from around the world.

“Born and raised within earshot of Badshahi Mosque, my mission is to reveal the soul of Pakistan beyond generic guidebooks. When you walk with Past Enlighten, you don't travel as a tourist. You explore as a welcomed guest into centuries of heritage, poetry, art and flavours.”

Bilawal, founder of Past Enlighten

Who we are

Past Enlighten is a small Lahore tour company based on Fort Road in the Walled City. Bilawal grew up in the old city around the Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort, and leads our Lahore heritage tours himself. For journeys further afield, he works with regional guides who know their valleys, routes and local customs.

Our flagship Lahore full-day tour is listed on TripAdvisor as the Lahore Heritage and Sightseeing Guided Tour.

How we guide

  • Stories, not just sights

    The history, legends and daily life behind each place: royal baths, painted mosques, old bazaars and the people who keep them alive.

  • Planned around you

    Private routes for solo travellers, families and photographers. We set the pace, timing and pickup to suit you.

  • English, Urdu & Punjabi

    Tours in English, Urdu or Punjabi, with help understanding local languages in the north such as Pashto, Shina, Burushaski and Balti.

  • Clear prices

    We confirm the price, group size, pickup and everything included in writing before you pay.

Where we take you

Lahore's Walled City, Lahore Fort and Badshahi Mosque, Sufi shrines and bazaars; Islamabad and Rawalpindi; and northern journeys to Naran and Kaghan, Swat, Neelum Valley, Hunza, Skardu and Deosai, Fairy Meadows and our 16-day Grand Northern tour. See all tours.
