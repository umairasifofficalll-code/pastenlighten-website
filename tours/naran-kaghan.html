About this tour

Celebrated in classic Sufi poetry for the folklore of Prince Saif-ul-Malook and the fairy Badi-ul-Jamal.

Highlights include Glacial lake Saif-ul-Malook beneath Malika Parbat, Babusar Pass viewpoint at 13,691 ft, and trout rafting along River Kunhar.
