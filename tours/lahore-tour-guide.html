Why travel with a local guide in Lahore

Lahore is a city of layers: Mughal emperors, Sikh rulers, the British Raj and a thousand years of bazaars and shrines, all within a few lanes of each other. A good guide turns a list of monuments into a story, finds the quiet courtyard behind a busy gate, and makes sure you eat in the right place.

Our founder Bilawal grew up around the Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort. He and our team guide in English, Urdu and Punjabi, and every tour is private for you and your group unless you choose a shared small group.

What your guide takes care of

  • The route and timing, so you see Lahore Fort, the Badshahi Mosque and the Walled City at the best hours
  • Entry tickets at the monuments on our tours (included in the price)
  • The history behind each place: Mughal, Sikh and colonial Lahore, told as stories
  • Good local food stops, from Fort Road to the old city’s chai and sweet shops
  • Practical help: dress codes for mosques and shrines, safe transport, photos of your group

Popular ways to tour Lahore with us

  • Lahore Fort & Walled City heritage walk: half day on foot through Delhi Gate, Wazir Khan Mosque, Lahore Fort and the Badshahi Mosque
  • Lahore full-day tour with the Wagah Border ceremony: the main sights plus the flag ceremony at sunset, with a private car
  • Rangila rickshaw tour of the old city: a colourful ride through the bazaars
  • Night Sufi shrine tour and bazaar shopping tour

Questions travellers ask

How much does a private tour guide in Lahore cost?

Our half-day Lahore tours start from US$70 per person for a solo traveller, with entry tickets included. Groups pay less per person, and the full-day tour with a private car and the Wagah Border ceremony starts from US$135 per person. Prices for every group size are shown on each tour page.

Do your guides speak English?

Yes. All our tours are led by English-speaking guides, and we also guide in Urdu and Punjabi.

Can you pick me up from my hotel?

Yes. Hotel pickup in Lahore is included on the full-day tour, and you can add transport to the half-day tours when you book. Without transport, we meet at Fort Road Food Street, Gate No. 4, next to the Badshahi Mosque.

Is the tour private?

Yes, tours are private for you and your group by default. If you prefer, you can join a shared small group at the shared price.
