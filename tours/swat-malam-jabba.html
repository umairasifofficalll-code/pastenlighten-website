About this tour

Ancient Udyana ('The Garden') conquered by Alexander the Great in 327 BCE, with over 1,400 Buddhist monasteries.

Highlights include Malam Jabba ski slopes and chairlifts, Italian marble White Palace (Sufed Mahal), and Butkara stupa.
