Naran Kaghan Valley: 10 Places and Experiences to Plan For

A practical introduction to Naran, Kaghan, the Kunhar River, mountain viewpoints and side trips.

Naran and Kaghan Valley are among the best-known summer escapes in northern Pakistan. The route follows the Kunhar River through forested slopes, villages and mountain scenery, with opportunities to pause for short walks, viewpoints and local food. Conditions vary by season, so build flexibility into your plans rather than treating every stop as guaranteed.

What to see and how to plan

  1. 1

    1. Follow the Kunhar River

    The river accompanies much of the journey and makes a natural place for photo stops. Choose safe pull-offs well away from traffic and never step into fast-moving water simply for a better picture.

  2. 2

    2. Explore Naran Bazaar

    Naran is a useful base for meals, supplies, accommodation and arranging local transport. In peak season, book rooms early and confirm what is included before paying.

  3. 3

    3. Plan a Lake Saif-ul-Malook excursion

    The lake is a major side trip from Naran. Access and transport can change with weather and road conditions; confirm local advice on the day and allow extra time.

  4. 4

    4. Enjoy viewpoints along the valley

    The drive itself is part of the experience. Stop only where parking is safe and permitted, and avoid blocking narrow roads for photographs.

  5. 5

    5. Make time for Kaghan village

    Kaghan and nearby settlements offer a slower look at valley life. Be considerate when photographing residents, homes or private land.

  6. 6

    6. Consider Shogran and Siri Paye

    These are commonly planned as separate excursions in the wider region. Check vehicle requirements, weather and trail conditions before setting out.

  7. 7

    7. Try local food

    Look for freshly prepared regional meals and tea in established eateries. Ask about ingredients if you have dietary restrictions.

  8. 8

    8. Keep the itinerary realistic

    Mountain roads can be slow, especially in busy periods. Plan fewer stops per day instead of assuming map distances translate into quick travel.

  9. 9

    9. Pack for changing weather

    Even in summer, evenings can be cool and rain can arrive quickly. Bring layers, waterproof outerwear, comfortable shoes and any personal medicines.

  10. 10

    10. Leave the valley clean

    Carry rubbish back with you, use marked paths and respect riversides, forests and local communities.

Practical travel tips

  • Check road and weather conditions shortly before departure.
  • Confirm transport, accommodation, cancellation terms and inclusions in writing.
  • Carry cash for smaller businesses and keep a charged phone and offline map.
  • Avoid driving mountain roads when tired or in poor visibility.
