Gilgit-Baltistan

Gilgit Travel Guide: Attractions and Northern Pakistan Routes

Discover Gilgit’s main sights, market areas and role as a travel hub, with practical advice for onward routes to Hunza, Skardu and the wider region.

Gilgit is an important regional hub in Gilgit-Baltistan and a useful stop on journeys through northern Pakistan. Travellers use it to rest, arrange onward transport and explore nearby viewpoints before continuing toward Hunza, Skardu or other mountain destinations. Distances are long and road conditions can change, so plan with flexibility.

What to see in Gilgit

Allow time for a relaxed look around the town, local markets and nearby viewpoints. The experience is as much about everyday regional life and mountain surroundings as it is about ticking off monuments. Ask locally about access, cultural etiquette and current conditions.

Gilgit as a travel hub

Gilgit is commonly used as a connection point for journeys toward Hunza and the Karakoram Highway, while Skardu trips follow a different route. The best route depends on your itinerary, weather, road status and transport availability.

Planning a Hunza journey

If heading toward Hunza, confirm road and weather conditions and allow time for traffic, construction or stops. Popular viewpoints and attractions can take longer than expected, so avoid scheduling every activity to the minute.

Planning a Skardu journey

Skardu is not simply a quick side trip from Gilgit; road conditions and journey times need careful checking. Compare the available transport options and consider overnight stops when required. Confirm your return route before booking flights or other fixed connections.

Best time to visit

Seasonal conditions vary across the region. Summer is a popular period for many road trips, while spring and autumn can bring colder nights and different access conditions. Winter travel may require additional preparation and may limit some excursions.

Local transport and services

Arrange reputable transport for mountain routes and clarify what is included in the fare. Carry cash for smaller purchases, keep offline maps available and ask accommodation staff about current road updates. Mobile signal and services may be intermittent outside town.

Packing and altitude

Pack layers, sun protection, comfortable walking shoes, water and any medication you need. If travelling to higher-altitude areas, pace yourself and pay attention to symptoms such as headache, nausea or unusual fatigue.

Sample two-day stopover

Use the first day to arrive, settle in and explore nearby town sights. Keep the second day flexible for a local excursion or onward travel preparation. If your main goal is Hunza or Skardu, treat Gilgit as a useful base rather than trying to cover every region in a short visit.

Plan your Pakistan trip

Travel conditions, opening times and access can change. Confirm current details before departure and allow flexibility for weather or road delays. Past Enlighten can help you discuss routes and trip timing.
