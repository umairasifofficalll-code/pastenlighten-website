Lahore Walled City: 10 Special Things

The Walled City is the old heart of Lahore, once enclosed by a wall with thirteen gates. Today it is a living maze of lanes, bazaars, mosques and havelis that is best explored on foot with a local guide.

Here are ten things that make it special, roughly in the order of a walking route that starts at Delhi Gate.

Top 10 at the Walled City

  1. 1

    Delhi Gate

    The main eastern entrance to the old city and the usual starting point for a walking tour.

  2. 2

    Shahi Hammam

    The Royal Bath, built in the 1630s, with painted domes and a heating system that was advanced for its time.

  3. 3

    Wazir Khan Mosque

    Famous for its tile work and painted interior, and considered by many to be the most beautiful building in Lahore.

  4. 4

    Sunehri Masjid (Golden Mosque)

    A small mosque with gilded domes, tucked into a busy bazaar near the old centre.

  5. 5

    Nau Nihal Singh Haveli

    A restored haveli with carved wooden balconies and painted rooms, showing how wealthy families once lived.

  6. 6

    Chowk Wazir Khan

    The lively square in front of the mosque, a good place to watch daily life and take a tea break.

  7. 7

    Kashmiri Bazaar

    One of the old bazaars, full of shops selling fabrics, jewellery and traditional goods.

  8. 8

    Narrow Lanes and Wooden Balconies

    Walk the lanes between old houses and notice the carved wooden jharokas that overhang the street.

  9. 9

    Roshnai Gate

    The gate linking the old city to the Hazuri Bagh area, between Lahore Fort and Badshahi Mosque.

  10. 10

    Fort Road Food Street

    End the day with Lahori dishes and a view of the lit-up Badshahi Mosque.

Visitor tips

  • Wear comfortable walking shoes: the lanes are narrow and sometimes uneven.
  • October to March is the most comfortable season for walking the old city.
  • Mornings are quieter in the bazaars; evenings are lively around Food Street.
  • A local guide helps you find the lesser-known havelis and avoid getting lost.
